Human Oversight Requirements for AI Decisions (AIGOV-005)

High-risk AI decisions requiring mandatory human review before action:
- Credit and loan decisions affecting >£10,000
- Hiring and employment decisions
- Healthcare triage and treatment recommendations
- Insurance underwriting
- Law enforcement risk assessments
- Benefits eligibility determinations

Human reviewers must:
- Complete AI literacy training (minimum 8 hours annually)
- Receive model output, confidence score, and top feature contributions
- Have authority to override the model recommendation
- Document their review rationale

Prohibited: fully automated decisions on the above categories without human review.
