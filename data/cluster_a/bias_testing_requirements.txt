Bias and Fairness Testing Requirements (AIETHICS-004)

Mandatory tests before any production deployment:
- Demographic parity: prediction rates equal across protected groups
- Equalized odds: equal true positive and false positive rates across groups
- Counterfactual fairness: outcomes unchanged when protected attributes changed

Protected attributes: gender, age, ethnicity, disability status, religion, national origin.

Approved tools: IBM AI Fairness 360, Microsoft Fairlearn, Aequitas.
Threshold: <2% disparity for high-risk models (credit, hiring, healthcare).
<5% disparity for lower-risk models.

Test data must include representative samples from all demographic groups.
Results must be reviewed by an independent assessor before sign-off.
