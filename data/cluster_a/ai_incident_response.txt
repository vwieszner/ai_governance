AI Model Incident Response Procedure (AIOPS-003)

Severity 1 (discriminatory outputs, financial harm, safety risk):
- Page on-call AI governance lead within 15 minutes
- Suspend model within 1 hour
- Notify CISO and Legal within 2 hours
- Incident report within 24 hours
- Post-mortem within 5 business days

Severity 2 (accuracy degradation >5% from baseline, unexpected behaviour):
- Notify model owner and team lead within 4 hours
- Remediation plan within 24 hours
- Resolution within 5 business days

All incidents logged in the AI Incident Register (internal.wiki/ai-incident-register).
Root cause must be documented. Patterns reviewed quarterly by AI Governance Board.
