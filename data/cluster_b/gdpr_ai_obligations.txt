GDPR and Automated Decision-Making (GDPR-003)

Article 22 rights for individuals:
- Right not to be subject to solely automated decisions with significant effects (credit, employment, health)
- Right to human review of automated decisions
- Right to explanation: meaningful information about logic, significance, and consequences
- Right to contest the decision

Obligations for AI systems making Article 22 decisions:
1. Disclose that a decision is automated (before processing)
2. Provide clear explanation of key factors and their weight
3. Implement human review process with genuine reconsideration
4. Document the logic in a form understandable to non-technical staff

Training data obligations:
- Data minimisation: collect only data necessary for the specified purpose
- Purpose limitation: do not repurpose training data for other models without consent
- Data subject rights apply to personal data used in training

Consult DPO (dpo@example.com) before any new AI processing involving personal data.
Run Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) for high-risk AI processing.
