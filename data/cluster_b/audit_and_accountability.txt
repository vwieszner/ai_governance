AI Audit and Accountability Requirements (AUD-004, AIGOV-005)

Audit log requirements:
- Capture: inputs, outputs, decision context, timestamp, user/system ID
- Retention: minimum 6 months for high-risk systems, 10 years for regulated sectors
- Protection: write-once (WORM) storage, tamper-evident
- Access: CISO approval required; access logged

Role accountability under EU AI Act:
- AI Provider: builds and deploys the model. Responsible for conformity assessment, technical documentation, post-market monitoring.
- AI Deployer: uses the model in a specific context. Responsible for fundamental rights impact assessment, human oversight implementation, incident reporting.
- AI User: end person affected. Has rights to explanation, human review, and redress.

Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment required for deployers of high-risk AI before deployment.
Results must be communicated to the AI provider and documented in the deployment register.
