AI Risk Assessment Framework (RISK-007)

Mandatory before deployment, updated annually or after significant model changes.

Risk dimensions to assess:
1. Purpose and context: Who makes decisions? Who is affected? What are the stakes?
2. Data quality and bias: Representativeness, historical bias, data gaps by demographic group
3. Harm potential: Individual harm (financial, physical, reputational), societal harm (discrimination, polarisation)
4. Security vulnerabilities: Adversarial attacks, data poisoning, model inversion
5. Transparency: Explainability of outputs, auditability of decisions
6. Mitigation measures: Technical and organisational controls in place

High-risk systems require independent auditor review of the assessment.
Results must be documented in the AI Risk Register and reviewed by the AI Governance Board.
Risk register accessible to all stakeholders via the GRC platform.
