EU AI Act — Requirements for High-Risk AI Systems (EUREG-001, effective August 2024)

High-risk AI categories (Annex III):
- Biometric identification and categorisation
- Critical infrastructure management
- Educational and vocational training access
- Employment, workers management, self-employment
- Access to essential private/public services (credit, insurance)
- Law enforcement
- Migration, asylum, border control
- Administration of justice and democratic processes

Obligations for providers of high-risk AI:
1. Register in the EU database before market placement
2. Conformity assessment (internal or third-party)
3. Technical documentation maintained for 10 years
4. Human oversight mechanisms enabling intervention
5. Post-market monitoring plan with annual reporting
6. Incident reporting to national authority within 15 days of serious incident

Prohibited practices (Article 5): social scoring by public authorities, real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces (with exceptions), subliminal manipulation, exploitation of vulnerable groups.
