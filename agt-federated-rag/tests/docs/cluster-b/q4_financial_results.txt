1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Q4 2025 was a record quarter for ACME Corp. Total revenue reached $48.3M, up 34%
year-over-year and 9% quarter-over-quarter, driven by strong enterprise ARR expansion
and the successful launch of the ACME Pro tier in October 2025.

Full Year 2025 revenue: $162.7M (guidance was $155-160M — beat at the top end).
EBITDA margin improved to 18.2% for the full year, up from 11.4% in FY2024.

2. Q4 2025 REVENUE BREAKDOWN

  SaaS Subscriptions (ARR):         $38.4M  (+38% YoY)
    — Starter tier:                  $6.1M
    — Growth tier:                   $14.8M
    — Enterprise tier:               $17.5M  (+51% YoY — key growth driver)

  Professional Services:             $5.9M   (+12% YoY)
  Marketplace / Integrations rev:    $4.0M   (+89% YoY — new in H2 2025)

  Total Q4 Revenue:                 $48.3M
