ACME CORP — DATA RETENTION POLICY
Document ID: POL-001
Classification: Internal — All Staff
Last Revised: 2026-01-15
Owner: Legal & Compliance Team

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines how ACME Corp retains, archives, and destroys data across all
business systems. It applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors
who handle company data.

2. RETENTION SCHEDULES

2.1 Customer Data
  - Active customer records: retained for the duration of the contract plus 7 years.
  - Inactive customer records: retained for 3 years after last transaction, then deleted.
  - Customer communications (email, chat): retained for 5 years.
  - Payment card data: not stored; handled exclusively by PCI-DSS certified processors.

2.2 Employee Data
  - Payroll records: retained for 7 years after employment ends.
  - Performance reviews: retained for 5 years after employment ends.
  - Recruitment records (unsuccessful candidates): retained for 12 months, then deleted.
  - Health and benefits records: retained for 6 years.

2.3 Financial Records
  - General ledger, invoices, purchase orders: retained for 7 years.
  - Tax records: retained for 10 years.
  - Expense reports: retained for 5 years.

2.4 Technical / Operational Data
  - Server and application logs: retained for 90 days online, 1 year cold storage.
  - Security event logs (SIEM): retained for 2 years.
  - Backup snapshots: daily backups kept for 30 days; monthly snapshots for 1 year.
  - Source code repositories: retained indefinitely (version-controlled).

3. GDPR / PRIVACY OBLIGATIONS
Data that falls under GDPR or local privacy regulation must not be retained beyond
its stated purpose. Upon a verified Subject Access Request or Right to Erasure
request, personal data must be purged within 30 days from all live systems and
within 90 days from backup archives.

4. DESTRUCTION PROCEDURES
  - Digital data: overwritten using NIST 800-88 Rev.1 media sanitisation guidelines.
  - Physical media (hard drives, USB): cross-cut shredding by certified vendor.
  - Paper records: cross-cut shredding on-site or via certified off-site service.

5. EXCEPTIONS
Exceptions to scheduled destruction must be approved in writing by the Chief Legal
Officer and recorded in the Data Retention Exception Register (DMS-REG-007).
Legal hold overrides any scheduled destruction for the duration of the hold.

6. RESPONSIBILITIES
  - Data Owners: ensure their team's data is classified and tagged correctly in the DMS.
  - IT Operations: enforce automated deletion schedules and maintain audit logs.
  - Legal & Compliance: maintain the exception register and handle DSARs.
  - All staff: report suspected violations to compliance@acmecorp.example.

7. REVIEW CYCLE
This policy is reviewed annually or following a material regulatory change.
